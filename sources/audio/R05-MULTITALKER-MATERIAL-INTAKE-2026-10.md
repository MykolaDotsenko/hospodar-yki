## 3. Original recording plan — high-value content first, NOT fake ready assets

**Proposed mini-pack A: 12 recorded episodes, 3+ consenting Finnish voices, first 2–4 minutes per scene, 3 phonetic contrasts with verified source audio, transcripts/timestamps/answers QA; all sizes/durations are budget hypotheses.** Tasks: huoltoyhtiö call, missed bus, health appointment, customer return, work shift, job interview, daycare message, housing charges, pharmacy question, public office request, train platform update, neighbour request. Script originals under [R05 study](../../research/pedagogy/32-r05-multitalker-phonetic-transfer-2026-10.md); no downloaded source voice is implicitly used. Assess B1/B2 suitability per actual task with S2 experts; no reuse of protected test prompts.

**Proposed mini-pack B: 40 short Finnish phonetic pairs in 3 voices** with normal natural delivery and new-voice held-out: long vs short vowels and geminates / word emphasis; choose only valid words/context pairs after qualified S2 review. *40* is production target, not acquired data; a new first-party recording is required.

**Validation:** For each file save source, legal rights, transcript/permission, author/voice release, original/reencoded derivatives, metadata, levels, codec, actual length, SHA-256, audio QA, learner answer criteria and hidden transfer items. Rights restricted content never committed to public Git. S2 reviewed only after actual human review, not from our prose.

## 4. 3–4GB and duplicate protection

User's capacity is a **maximum allowable installation size**, not a target to fill with raw corpora. Prior approved full-offline **install-time** route remains; [R05 detailed provisional 3,600MB plan](../../research/pedagogy/32-r05-multitalker-phonetic-transfer-2026-10.md) and [ADR-023](../../docs/23-android-offline-game-and-3gb-media-architecture.md). Do not put pending URLs in assets/audio/library.json or count them as physical files. Don't count the same voice as new talker due to segments/transcoding. No unlicensed songs, proprietary lesson audio, personal speaker corpora, scraped exam prompts or Yle recordings.

**Release gate:** zero source-only URL interpreted as installed asset; zero unreviewed/redistribution-blocked asset packaged; all actual imports require SHA256 and per-file rights, native-playback/offline check, pre-release S2/learning quality and technical metadata. **R05 material sourcing completed at research stage, but 0 new binaries imported by this PR.**
