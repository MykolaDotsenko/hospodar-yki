## Build into our LEARN flow
- For **puhekieli**, compare authentic conversational forms with written equivalents; learner must generate a new sentence rather than only watch.
- For **quantity and prosody**, listen, choose minimal-pair interpretation, speak aloud, and revisit with a different unseen recording after 7/14 days.
- For **mielipide** and **tapaamisen sopiminen**, provide a new prompt, make user produce Finnish before revealing models.
- For **reaction** videos, require a spoken/text reply and a later unpredictable variant; never mark unreviewed speech with official B1/YKI score.
