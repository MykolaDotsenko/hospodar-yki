# Knowledge sources, corpora, dictionaries and course references

**Content vs reference:** learning activities belong to [learning/exercises](../learning/exercises/README.md); vocabulary and grammar lookup datasets belong to [dictionaries](../docs/FOLDER-STRUCTURE.md). Books and external PDFs live under [knowledge/books/](books/README.md) with their own origin and license terms, not automatically bundled into the app.

Reading and official sources: [docs/03 resources](sources/03-resources-books-courses.md), [docs/06 sources](sources/06-sources-and-verification.md), [docs/10 corpora](sources/10-open-corpora-yki-2026-10.md), [research source register](../research/yki/yki-content-sources-2026-10.json).

When adding a knowledge reference record creator/source URL/DOI, year/version, language, license, whether user-facing/bundlable vs link-only, methodology limits, date verified, and where it supports a pedagogical rule. Do not copy protected course/key content. For every externally sourced PDF, distinguish storing a research copy from permissions to package/re-distribute in Android/Web.
