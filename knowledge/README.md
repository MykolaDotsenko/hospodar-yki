# Knowledge — dictionaries and locally stored books

**Content vs reference:** learning activities belong to [learning/exercises](../learning/exercises/README.md); vocabulary and grammar lookup datasets belong to [dictionaries](dictionaries/README.md). Books and external PDFs live under [knowledge/books/](books/README.md) with their own origin and license terms, not automatically bundled into the app.

External learning resources (stored in [sources/learning](../sources/learning/README.md)): [docs/03 resources](../sources/learning/03-resources-books-courses.md), [docs/06 sources](../sources/learning/06-sources-and-verification.md), [docs/10 corpora](../sources/learning/10-open-corpora-yki-2026-10.md), [research source register](../research/yki/yki-content-sources-2026-10.json).

When adding dictionaries, record version, corpus, source, authorship, license, coverage and human-review status. External references and reading lists belong in [sources/learning](../sources/learning/README.md). Do not copy protected course/key content. For every externally sourced PDF, distinguish storing a research copy from permissions to package/re-distribute in Android/Web.
