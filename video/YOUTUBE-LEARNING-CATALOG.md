## Selection principles

- **Highest value:** active comprehension and independent productive responses (YKI speaking situations/opinions, writing, practical Finnish listening), `puhekieli ↔ kirjakieli`, work/housing/health, personal weak grammar (illatiivi, partitiivi).
- **Balanced learning:** real contexts + slow/fast forms, original writing/speaking before exposure to a published model answer, gist before details, delayed unseen retest. Long lectures are indexed by useful chapters.
- **Source quality:** direct video URL was found with a matching title, creator, description, and sometimes publisher's chapter timestamps. This is **metadata verification**, not proof that each video was watched end-to-end, is currently embeddable, has accurate subtitles, or is teacher-reviewed.
- **Score /100:** editorial product-fit estimate based on learning usefulness, not scientific effectiveness or measured YKI scores.
- **Exam honesty:** videos titled YKI are **third-party practice**, not OPH/JYU official digital exams. They are appropriate **only for LEARN**; model answers may be visible in the YouTube video and its chapters, so none belongs in the unseen exam bank.

## Future YouTube player — acceptance specification (`YTP-01…14`)

1. **YTP-01:** support content providers `github_asset` (licensed video already in repo) and `youtube` (watch URL/videoId only) without conflating file state. Provider normalized as `{provider,media_id,watch_url,chapters,source_attribution}`.
2. **YTP-02:** use the **official YouTube embedded/IFrame Player API** (if allowed for that video), not `<video src="youtube…">`, proxy/cache, downloading or extracted MP3. Keep player identity and attribution.
3. **YTP-03:** deferred third-party embed until explicit user **Play**/consent, thumbnail placeholder + privacy notice; allow **Open on YouTube** fallback when embedded playback fails or embedding disabled; no invented live playability flag.
4. **YTP-04:** keep publisher controls, overlay rules, ads and attribution intact; no hiding/changing YouTube advertisements or audio-only/background extraction. Our lesson prompts appear **outside** the player.
5. **YTP-05:** use chapter `start_seconds` for navigation, end only when permitted by official API; never claim source-defined chapter ends are precise if the creator only supplied starts.
6. **YTP-06:** independent learning attempt **before** showing learner prompt's model answer when the video provides one. When the answer is embedded early in the clip, do **not** claim an unseen independent test; treat video as a learning demonstration.
7. **YTP-07:** `caption_status=null/pending` until independently reviewed; no claims of CC/VTT, no unauthorized copying/scraping. User can enable creator-provided captions via regular YouTube controls.
8. **YTP-08:** distinguish `A2–B1` target guess vs explicit publisher `B1` tag; teacher CEFR review can change level and score. Recheck deleted/age-restricted/geoblocked video on player error.
9. **YTP-09:** `learn` only until original teacher-approved assessment exists; `exam` strictly excludes YouTube external video and model-answer material unless officially authorized and verified.
10. **YTP-10:** record local lesson events (`attempt_id,video_id,segment_id,first_uncued_answer,hint_used,feedback_status`), no raw media bytes/video watch analytics or private audio uploaded by default.
11. **YTP-11:** accessible skip-to-exercise, links, keyboard controls around embeds; responsive 16:9; captions controls stay YouTube's; nonvideo text task fallback where needed.
12. **YTP-12:** protect users from answer leakage via optional `watch segment` guidance **outside the player**; don't programmatically obscure player buttons, block seeking, force completion or require quizzes to keep watching.
13. **YTP-13:** technical compliance gate: verify current [YouTube IFrame Player API](https://developers.google.com/youtube/iframe_api_reference), [API Developer Policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies), [Required Minimum Functionality](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality), plus GDPR/consent, `Referer` client identification and privacy requirements. Re-evaluate when implementing.
14. **YTP-14:** periodically verify links/availability (only via approved interfaces); on broken links mark `unavailable` without losing archived pedagogical notes. Do not use private scraping mechanisms.

**Content-readiness is not playback-readiness.** Implement only after testing player behavior on desktop/mobile and verifying embedding allowed on each video. No server-side proxy/cache of YouTube AV or unpermitted downloads.

## Sample lesson design

**Learn → Kuunteleminen → Lauran päivä, osa 1**:

- independent gist from `start=01:11` to `04:35` without pre-supplied transcript;
- compare informal Finnish section `04:35–07:24` and identify genuine `puhekieli` forms;
- give a Finnish response `Mitä Laura tekee aamulla?` before opening a model;
- feedback from original teacher-review-approved lesson content; record assisted vs independent separately;
- revisit a **different** situation/audio after 7 days with no support; **do not** report a YKI score from watching a video.

**Important:** YouTube videos may include on-screen text or spoken example answers; **we have inspected publisher metadata, not fully audited the spoken contents**. Such clips need viewing and human S2 evaluation before their scoring mode is enabled.
