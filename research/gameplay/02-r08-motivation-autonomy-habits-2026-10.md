## 1. Main decision: meaningful persistence, NOT DAU, XP or perfect calendar

**PICOT:** Adult Finnish learners randomly assigned to an **autonomy-supportive mission/recovery layer** (choose real-life goal, optional short 5/10/20-min plan, meaning-first feedback about verifiable learning progress, gentle return after gaps, no loss framing) vs a **strong** neutral curated Finnish practice interface with the same time, S2 content, audio, independent feedback and spaced reviews. Primary **day-14 blind scored unseen real-world Finnish speaking+writing task success**, separate reading/listening; day-30, practice adherence, autonomy, burden, dropout secondary; pre-register attrition and rights. **Trial not run**.

**ADOPT P0:** Learner autonomy, true skill-specific effort and independent evidence, optional relevant 2D narrative, explicit opt-out, privacy and no pressure/losing points. **ADAPT P1:** If–then cue, short daily routine, local return invitation and optional nonpunitive progress. **EXPERIMENT P1:** Badge, optional social peer, subtle completion animation and sound only if a delayed language endpoint improves vs a fair comparator. **REJECT P0:** punitive streak reset, forced daily minimum, public ranking of Finnish proficiency, emotional FOMO, pay-to-win XP or artificially withholding curriculum, default notification spam, "B1 92%".

We **do not** assert that every streak/competition causes harm: controlled 2025 education and 2026 language-app studies demonstrate engagement benefits, but outcomes, incentives and harm differ. Hospodar may offer optional personal calendar highlights in a later A/B pilot if nonpunitive, opt-out and beneficial; they cannot function as an obligatory gate.

**Bias/transfer appraisal:** We did not perform full PRISMA, quantitative meta-pooling, per-source GRADE or independent replication. E01/E02 overlap likely; E05 review and E03 meta may overlap individual papers. High RCT N of E07 does **not** compensate for selected outcome subsample, and E08 is a contest engagement outcome, not mastery. Conflation of English vocab, Peruvian child mathematics and adult Finnish would be invalid. Scores like g, r are not % learning gain, YKI-level confidence or predicted passing probability.

## 3. Product design — explicit voluntary autonomy, competence, relatedness

### Autonomy (mandatory quality contract, P0)
- Onboarding asks **what learner genuinely needs to do in Finnish**: housing, work, children's school, health administrative dialog, shops, YKI. All options equally accessible offline; no dependence on learner race, ethnicity, intelligence or inferred stress.
- One optional goal for today, user-selected **5/10/20 minutes** (defaults to 5 minutes only if genuinely user chooses a plan; don't secretly enroll). User may change plan, skip, pause, or open plain no-game LEARN. No streak penalty or paid "restore".
- **If–then plan:** user supplies natural routine cue, e.g. "Коли закінчу обід, 5 хв потреную аудіювання з теми Житло." This is an invitation to re-engage, *not* a schedule that sends push messages. No assumption about learner sleep or working hours.
- Default notifications **OFF**. Future reminders require separate explicit opt-in, quiet hours/time-zone and easy revoke; no reminder tool, push scheduler or stored precise location in R08 reference.

### Competence — never fake it
- Show concrete action: "Ти самостійно зрозумів, що huoltaja прийде torstaina", if objective approved key or calibrated human review. "Ти практикував говоріння" for **ungraded speaking**, never "Твоє говоріння B1 92%".
- R03 morphology (vesi→vedessä) can show **first independently correct grammatical form** as a separate, clearly marked domain outcome, not automatically "writing level" or YKI success.
- Count independent attempts and feedback repairs separately. Learner can view successful *new-context* attempts, dated retention tasks, observed change (baseline vs held-out) only after real S2 blind rating. No click-bait unlock trophies.
- Missed reviews appear as "є 3 заплановані повторення" — never "ти втратив 3 дні"; avoid unbounded catch-up queue.

### Relatedness — no public performance shame
- Optional **real-world roleplay** (huoltoyhtiö, päivähoito, työvuoro) or consented peer conversation; no public profile/league ranks. Two people can collaboratively complete a functional Finnish situation **without** their grades ranking.
- A quiet optional narrative world can illustrate real-world social meaning and agency: Finn dialogue, friendly NPC, purposeful task, transfer to another interlocutor; art/2D/voices do NOT automatically make language skill better.
- Protect user choice of no social layer, no voice sharing, no opponent countdown, no push/persistent login.

### Sensory design and 3–4 GB total-app planning
- Reuse R05 warm Nordic editorial palette and Ukrainian identity **only for meaningful route/navigation milestones**. Resting/return message is calm warm paper/ink and one factual goal; no red "streak lost", guilt animation or urgent sound.
- Three sound classes: **language audio** (priority, user starts, rights-cleared), **quiet semantic environmental sound** (optional, disabled during language speech and human recording), **completion SFX** (OFF by default, accessibility alternative text). No product evidence that the "correct frequency" helps learning.
- 3–4GB full offline Android *allowed planning cap*, not a mandate to fill with motivational cutscenes. Add rich content ONLY if it leads to meaningful independent Finnish performance and licensing clear, PAD install-time future integration; no media added in this R08 PR. EXAM remains no world, no reward, no adaptive streak and no invented 2027 digital exam behavior.

## 4. Concrete 4-skill mission recipes and ethical feedback

| Skill | Voluntary micro-mission | Useful result vs tempting but misleading gamified score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reading** | 5 min: read a *taloyhtiö* note, answer when water is unavailable; new notice next week. | Correct independent comprehension recorded; avoid "+100 XP" on opened PDF. |
| **Listening** | 5 min: listen to different licensed human-voice huoltoyhtiö voicemail and identify actual time. | Independent audio-only response, feedback, 14-day new speaker; optional caption marks supported. |
| **Writing** | 10 min: send a polite Finnish childcare appointment message with new constraints; optional guided repair then novel recipient. | Submitted writing = **ungraded**; trained S2 humans needed for actual communicative quality, no length-based B1 score. |
| **Speaking** | 5 min: reply aloud "En pääse torstaina, mutta perjantai sopii". Voluntary recording with separate permission and fallback typing. | Speaking practice logged but ungraded; no forced mic, ASR CEFR prediction or automatic XP-for-minutes. |

**Comeback screen example after gap:**
> "Можна просто продовжити з однієї короткої вправи. Нічого надолужувати не потрібно." 
> [5 хв аудіювання · житло] [Обрати інше] [Без гри]

This is a **UX copy proposal**, not a tested motivational intervention or active Hospodar screen.

## 6. Controlled experiments (future proposals, NOT preregistered, NOT run)

**R08-MOT-H01 (P0)** autonomy/meaning vs neutral strong learning UI: A optional real-life goals+5/10/20 min plus calm progress; B identical S2 lessons/feedback/time + clear neutral task browser, no imposed rewards. **Primary day-14 human-blind scored unseen Finnish writing + speaking** outcomes (independent each), reading/listening separate; 30-day durability and attrition. Secondary self-reported SDT autonomy/competence, task burden, voluntary return. Don't claim power N without review.

**H02 (P1)** optional specific if–then plan vs equally simple self-selected reminder-less routine list; randomized micro-trial with adult Finnish learners, timestamps only with consent; **primary actual day-14 transfer**; next-day return secondary; adjust exposure time and check missing reviews. E11/E12 conflicting evidence implies possible null result.

**H03 (P1)** factual "observed success" messages vs neutral task-completion explanation with same independent evidence. Outcome **human-rated novel communication** and accuracy, compare motivational pressure/worry; no fabricated success from ungraded speaking.

**H04 (P1)** optional private nonpunitive regularity calendar vs no calendar, identical task/curriculum/feedback and controls. Measure retention of learned Finnish and time-on-task, not just consecutive-day metric; detect subgroup harm, break anxiety and coercion. Contradictory E07/E06 means do not prejudge streak outcomes.

**H05 (P1)** purposeful low-art 2D life scene (with one NPC and actual Finnish turn) vs plain accessible equivalent, matched text/audio/time and independent response; novel day14 speaking transfer; battery/performance and sensory overload secondary. Prevent novelty confounding and verify media rights.

**H06 (P2)** opt-in cooperative peer roleplay vs private fictional NPC, with equivalent Finnish task duration and rights/consent, human blind scoring, no performance rankings and safe data deletion. Avoid publicly storing voice.

**H07 (P2)** silent/quiet motivation micro-feedback vs optional single brief muted completion sound after session (no overlap with Finnish speech); measure independent retention, annoyance and accessibility harms. If no benefit, reject assets.

**Study gates:** independent baseline, randomized stratification, frozen source/S2/media versions and held-out unfamiliar tasks/voices, active time-matched comparator and blinded human R04 rubric, privacy/consent, preregister primary outcomes/power/attrition/multiplicity before run. Measure sign+size+CI, report null and harm. No inference that more DAU ≡ more Finnish competence.

## 7. R08-MOT acceptance gates (proposed future product requirements, NOT validated)

1. Mode strict LEARN only; no EXAM promotion/pressure, no official score.
2. Learner explicitly chooses actual Finnish goal, skill and time; accessible "skip" and "no game" paths.
3. Default 0 notifications, 0 invasive tracking; future opt-in only with revoke.
4. A pause/missed day never resets earned knowledge, locks content, imposes catch-up quota or uses shame language.
5. No automatic public standings, social comparisons or pay-to-win access gates.
6. No XP/streak/notification metric misrepresented as CEFR/YKI improvement.
7. Only first independent objective or qualified human review counts as verified task success.
8. Ungraded writing/speaking never transformed into a performance score; practice exposure remains visible and valuable.
9. Guided repair never overrides original independent error evidence.
10. Local date processing robust across daylight saving changes and invalid future dates.
11. User plan and copy stored only if explicit consent to local settings; export/edit/delete controls when integrated.
12. Respect device accessibility: readable focus, 320px mobile, 200% zoom, reduced motion and no mandatory sound.
13. Any environment sound or completion SFX never overlays Finnish audio or microphone.
14. Offline-first: optional 3–4GB media packs rights-cleared and not required for core motivation loop; no cloud AI dependency.
15. Time-bounded messages, no notification or leaderboarding in EXAM.
16. Stable deterministic choices, bounded saved event histories and duplicate event prevention.
17. Comparable independent day14/day30 communication outcome with quality checks, user autonomy and harms reported.
18. Separate production PR with Web+Android real QA, persistence migration, S2-approved copy and rollback; no false "already deployed" claim.
