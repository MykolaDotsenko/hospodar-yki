# Visual design evaluation — premium Ukrainian identity without cognitive overload

**Status:** proposed review/testing checklist, not user-tested validation. [Design sources](README.md), [brand tokens](../../brand/tokens.json).

## Design criteria

- Recognizable Hospodar "threshold → route → action" grammar, meaningful Ukrainian cultural elements without stereotypes or pseudo-official Finnish/YKI insignia.
- Tasks in Finnish are the visual focus; optional Ukrainian explanatory layer never competes with reading the Finnish sentence.
- Colors emphasize skill grouping, action state and hierarchy—not unproven universal "memory colors". Maintain non-color cues and measured readable contrast.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, no decorative game HUD in EXAM, no distracting animated timer effects.
- Photos and illustrative source art need documented rights, creator, `alt_fi`/`alt_uk` or `decorative`, resolutions/formats and target delivery.
- Measure mobile 360px, tablet, desktop: legibility, navigation, caption placement, speech interactions, failure screens and Web/Android parity.

## Evidence

Maintain design hypotheses with a link to screenshot/build commit, usability task, specific success metric, observed results and regression test. Track a color attention claim as `research_inference` until a controlled study demonstrates effect in Hospodar's learner population.
