## 1. Concrete research question and top-line conclusion

**PICOT:** For adult Ukrainian-L1 Finnish A2→B1 learners, under equal S2-curated content, identical recording/visuals, equal effective study time and feedback, does **user-controlled semantic visual/audio presentation + independent-first answer + staged meaning-first corrective feedback + self-repair + novel context** outperform the same high-quality conventional interface (simple playable authentic clip + structured recall + same feedback and repair but without staged interaction)? Primary outcome is **blind-rated day-14 independently completed novel Finnish communicative task**, measured separately for writing, speaking, reading/listening and transfer between unfamiliar voices/contexts; day-30 secondary. Hospodar experiment **NOT RUN**.

**Scientific judgment:** Meaningful production and feedback matter more than unlimited cosmetic motion. Instructional games, audiovisual input and L2 dialogue tools show promise, but their pooled results **do not isolate a particular interface widget** and **do not imply Finnish YKI success**. Colors/audio style are only hypotheses; active answer-first contract and WCAG are immediate design safety requirements.

**Decision:** ADOPT/P0 **user control, answer sealed until submission, real optional audio and low distraction, semantic aid on demand and transparent "assisted" evidence label**. ADAPT/P0 constrained interaction scenes with S2-reviewed linguistic goals. EXPERIMENT/P1 embedded semantic diagrams, segment-bound questions and role turn-taking independently. REJECT/P0 auto-playing music, decorative click effects, involuntary spoken prompts, default timed animations, streak punishment, fake auto-graded speaking and leakage of digital-YKI scripts. RECHECK/P1 real 2027 EXAM demo behavior before asserting fidelity.

AA is release target, not achieved by writing this spec. |
| R07-E14 **W3C_STANDARD/AAA_GUIDE** | [Understanding 2.3.3 animation from interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions): AAA recommends turning off nonessential interaction motion, system reduced-motion. | **ADOPT P0 best practice** even where AAA not the formal AA requirement; static alternative to moving path semantics. |
| R07-E15 **GENERAL_REVIEW** | [2025 comprehensive review of video features](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00481-x): distinguishes audio, visual, textual, instructor, learner-control, interactive features, production style, instruction; methodological findings not single isolated app A/B. | **ADAPT P1:** feature-source matrix to prevent audio/caption/quiz/visual confounds; if adding a feature, record what stayed identical. |
| R07-E16 **L2_CONTROLLED_NO_STRONG_COMPARATOR** | [ASR Chinese dialogue platform 2025, 113 intermediate students / 9 roleplays](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10791-025-09787-9): significant pre-post speaking rise; willingness to communicate and anxiety associated with results, not isolated ASR vs matched human. | **RECHECK P1:** social comfort and voluntary repeat roleplay, no auto CEFR from ASR; compare learner-facing manual/peer dialogues without compulsory AI. |

**Synthesis warning:** these experiments and meta-analyses sometimes overlap primary studies and use different effect types (pre/post vs between-group), baselines, languages, ages and outcomes. Do **not** add their g values or convert them into % "more memorized". This is a selective source-based critical review, NOT an independently run PRISMA or individual GRADE. The product has NO learner-level R07 outcome yet.

## 3. R07 UX information architecture — stage *decisions* instead of cosmetic interactions

1. **Choose mission and 4-skill outcome (LEARN only):** exact objective ("зрозумій дату ремонту", not "збери 50 XP"), topic, estimated 5/10/20 min, user may skip/reroute; R06 recommender provides **reason code** and alternate option, not false mastery.
2. **Input stage:** show Finnish context in one panel; user-initiated 1× normal-speed voice (with pause, rewind and replay); semantic static context if answer not revealed. No click chirp or music overlap; audio transcript optionally available for accessibility as **supported attempt**.
3. **Independent first response:** writing/recording/type where appropriate. Do not reveal answer, colored case ending, pictorial key, transcript or text-to-speech target before user responds; app retains safe draft with real storage once integrated. Reading/listening objective questions may be scored with S2-approved key; writing/speaking always **ungraded** absent approved human rating.
4. **Meaning-first feedback:** show question meaning and explanation **only after first attempt**. If failed, offer optional scaffold, explain case/word ending and trigger guided repair; record first independent error separately, NEVER overwrite it by guided repair.
5. **Novel transfer:** different role/voice/situation; hide original key, subtitles and feedback; score only objectively validated answer or human rating, otherwise **ungraded**. Schedule 7/14/30-day unseen in R01/R05/R06.
6. **Reflect:** one precise action ("наступного разу потренуємо mennessä") and clear "review due"; no pressure, punitive loss of streak or dashboard magic skill score.

**Critical exception:** A learner with limited hearing, reading needs, attention needs or temporary inability to record can request captions, transcript or visual equivalent *before* independent first answer; provide educational access immediately and mark assessment evidence **supported**, NOT infer lower Finnish proficiency or deny task completion. EXAM accessibility must reflect confirmed official 2027 accommodations/implementation separately; don't silently import LEARN affordances or invalidate official simulation.

## 4. Proposed 4-skill Finnish design with task-owned semantics

| Skill | Meaningful interaction / Finnish example | Independent evidence; new context |
|---|---|---|
| Reading | Read a *taloyhtiö* notice about maintenance: "Vettä ei tule perjantaina klo 9–13"; learner identifies actual consequence and writes a short clarifying question. Visual timeline **after** first response. | Objective detail check can use reviewed answer key; novel building notice day14 and functional request ungraded/human-rated. |
| Listening | Human licensed Finnish voice, voicemail "Huoltaja tulee torstaina kahden jälkeen"; pause/play then independent day/time response; after answer show a timestamp timeline and caption. | No transcript before independent attempt unless learner requests support; unseen new voice/bus cancellation after 14 days, not same clip. |
| Writing | Write a response to daycare cancellation "En pääse hakemaan lasta ajoissa"; optional after-submission semantic/rektio hint, repair, new message to school with different relationship. | Preserve original draft; **never auto-label YKI 3 / B1** or score fluency from text length. R04 S2 blind rubric if available. |
| Speaking | Turn-taking role with service counter: "Voisinko vaihtaa vuoroani torstaille?" listener may replay on demand; explicit record/start/stop, permission and fallback typing if mic unavailable. | Voice recording ≠ ASR-based grade. Human reviewer or self-reflection, novel spoken role after 14 days; no interrupting sound. |

**One task card design, grounded in R05 palette not invented neuro-color:**
- Brand nav "threshold → route → action" at gateway; **language action first** in actual LEARN task. Warm paper #F8F5ED, ink #18251F and skill accent from tokens; verify exact contrast per combination, never color as sole signal.
- One clear Finnish sentence or useful semantic map above primary answer field; stage label is plain text plus step number; secondary button "Потрібна підказка" makes intent + changed independent status clear.
- Feedback uses semantic text and optional diagram AFTER first answer, not auto-revealed morphology.
- 180ms token for brief focus affordances is a brand direction, **not scientifically optimized duration**; reduced-motion alternative immediate.
- Sound effects off by default; all essential events receive textual/visual confirmation; "success" animation non-essential, under user control.
- Compact phone screen (320 CSS px), desktop (keyboard, captions, recording), 200% text zoom, iOS/Android screen reader, contrast 4.5:1 small text and 3:1 large/UI where WCAG applicable; no horizontal critical task overflow or input covered by virtual keyboard.

## 6. Engineering prototype — intentionally not an active UI, and what it proves

Reference [app/r07-interaction-core.mjs](../../app/r07-interaction-core.mjs) (pure deterministic event state machine) + [15 direct tests](../../app/tests/r07-interaction-core.test.mjs). Transitions:
- ready → independent via explicit START; audio never autoplays, and must be explicitly PLAY_FINNISH_AUDIO or user requests an accessible transcript before a listening answer.
- support request (transcript/meaning/slow audio/visual cue) marks answer as **assisted/ungraded**, without denying access or penalizing learning.
- first response → feedback; only S2-approved objective reading/listening answer keys generate binary correctness. Speaking/writing never machine-grade.
- optional guided repair → novel transfer; original answer and caption hidden during transfer; transfer never fake-graded.
- strict LEARN only; EXAM and unapproved/unknown-license/held-out task rejected; no browser APIs, no SFX calls, no active rendering.

**Limits of prototype:** not imported by app/app.mjs, does not render controls, synthesize audio, record microphone or handle durable atomic persistence; meaningful new transfer equality checked only by exact prompt string (production needs semantic/task-similarity/editorial QA). Prototype hard-codes quiet sound and passes reducedMotion UI preference, it does not *test actual WCAG compliance*. Objective L2 scoring must later support approved alternatives and punctuation/real native speech dialect; this is only a reference state machine, not a deployable grader. Listening without replay user choice and multiple speeds still needs actual UI/a11y tests. Human speech evidence remains ungraded even if transcribed.

## 7. R07 pre-registered study proposals (NOT RUN)

**R07-H01 (P0) feedback stage:** identical Finnish authentic task, content and tutoring feedback; A meaning-first staged reveal **after first independent response** vs B same depth feedback displayed together after response. Randomize/stratify A2–B1, equal time, objective first attempt; primary day14 new communicative oral/written meaning/form transfer, blind human S2 raters; confidence intervals, cognitive load and learner preference secondary. Detect potential harmful split-attention.
**R07-H02 (P0) listening pause prompt:** A voluntary replay and one semantic pause question vs B uninterrupted playback then equally active matched recall. Same speakers/audio, same total time and feedback. Day14 novel-speaker listening gist/detail; ensure pause doesn't trivialize known key. Check autonomy and cognitive load.
**R07-H03 (P1) semantic visual:** A *meaning-congruent* apartment/timetable/timeline near input vs B same semantic information in simple static text, match detail level/time. Outcome day14 unseen notice comprehension and independent written request; compare less proficient groups and visual accessibility.
**R07-H04 (P1) interactive role scene:** A task-driven branching dialog with one relevant choice+production vs B task-driven linear clip and equal productive turns. Match content/audio/time; primary human-rated new role speaking after 14/30 days, evaluate willingness to communicate and anxiety without assuming mental health diagnosis.
**R07-H05 (P1) optional calm sound and visual motion:** A optional restrained nonverbal orientation cue with explicit off switch vs B full silence/static. Both do *not* overlay Finnish speech; primary actual delayed listening/reading outcomes plus task abandon and a11y discomfort. Negative/zero effects are acceptable.
**R07-H06 (P2) aesthetic art direction:** current warm Nordic design vs stripped high-contrast equivalent with exact same Finnish text, controls, assets and duration; prefer within-person counterbalanced *only if* no learning carryover, otherwise between-groups. Primary delayed transfer, then choice, sustained engagement, readability; no "perfect color for memory" claim.
**R07-H07 (P1) 2D exploration tax:** B comparable non-game task, A low-density 2D playable scene; time/content matched and media rights cleared, primary day14 communicative Finnish performance and attrition; game XP only secondary and never a proxy.

**Protocol before recruitment:** explicitly freeze interface screenshots, Finnish source versions, feedback content, test items and delayed human rubrics; use strong active comparators instead of weak passive controls; control feature confounds and time on target; preregister primary outcome, minimum worthwhile gain, required power/sampling plan, moderators, missingness, accessibility harms and mixed null/positive findings. User research consent/privacy authorization first. No studies preregistered/running and no effect estimates from Hospodar.

## 8. R07 acceptance gates — NEW R7-01…R7-18, product gates remain unverified

1. Task learning objective observable in Finnish meaning use, not generic XP/dwell time.
2. Independent response recorded before feedback/hint/key/caption reveal; first-attempt distinction survives restarts.
3. Accessibility help may be requested pre-first-answer and marks *supported learning*, not inability or failed assessment.
4. Reading/listening structured review keyed only to S2-approved accepted variants; writing/speaking ungraded absent calibrated humans.
5. Pausing, replay, speed choice, transcript and safe quiet controls user controlled; no audio autoplay.
6. Novel transfer hides original answer, worked examples, overlaid colors and transcript.
7. Role dialog does not falsely claim natural Finnish human voice when synthetic; license rights and S2 review required.
8. Safe cold-start and selection from R06 with manual override; no coercive streak punishment.
9. LEARN/EXAM isolation; EXAM has no study aids, cues, reward SFX, adaptive question leaks.
10. All audio/video offline packs check hash/rights, unavailable media supports meaningful fallback where valid; no unknown-license re-hosting.
11. WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, touch, labels, keyboard focus, logical reading order; 200% zoom and 320px mobile no blocked input.
12. Reduced motion preference and static alternatives (2.3.3 AAA beneficial design); pause/stop/hide true W3C 2.2.2 implementation.
13. Text alternative to essential sound, no background music during any Finnish listening/speaking; sound effects off by default.
14. Heavy art and GPU-free fallback: p95 interaction performance on mid-range Android in airplane mode; no arbitrary fixed budget without baseline.
15. Plain readable typography from brand tokens; never color-only correctness or decorative UI behind text.
16. Human outcome/experience and harms evaluated with equal-time strong comparator, blinded day14/30 transfer, explicitly report null.
17. Event state changes deterministic and unit-tested (prototype tests not equivalent to browser/mobile compliance).
18. Distinct production feature PR with UI+storage+CI+manual browser+Android accessibility verification; rollback and migration if needed.
