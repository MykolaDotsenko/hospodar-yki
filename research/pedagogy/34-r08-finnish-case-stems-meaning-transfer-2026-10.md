## 1. Scientific question / highest-confidence decision

**PICO:** Compared with an **equally intensive, well-edited Finnish grammar training control** offering the same examples, amount of exposure, time, feedback and practice, does the **explicit meaning-based case decision → independent stem production → focused feedback → guided repair → independently new use** lead to higher blind-rated success in **unseen B1 functional communication after 14 days**? Distinguish outcome of *interpreting a case* (receptive) from producing a correct stem (morphological production) and from successful full writing/speaking to a new interlocutor (farther transfer). Secondary 30-day retention, R04 human-rated novel writing/speaking, latency where feasible without inducing speed pressure. **Not tested in Hospodar.**

**ADOPT P0:** meaning before form, explicit editor-verified case/valency and inflection, first independent attempts immutable, graded form-vs-meaning separately, after-feedback visual stem cue optional. **EXPERIMENT P1:** do typed separate selection+production help vs a strong one-step exercise control. **REJECT P0:** suffix-only generator and generic regex diagnosis, "all -si becomes -de" universal lesson (different contexts!), automatic grades for free writing/speaking, uncertain key accepted as verified, 432Hz/music in grammatical audio, or mixing with EXAM.

## 3. R08 novel editorial bank: 14 original unreviewed draft tasks, NOT 14 published lessons

[Draft content JSON](../../learning/exercises/b1/grammar/r08-meaning-stems-original-drafts.json), **14 stable IDs**, fields:
- Finnish whole sentence and explicit lemma; Ukrainian semantic comprehension question; **controlled 2–3 competing semantic options**, e.g. location/toward/from, negated/completed object, rektio or state change. Answers may not be hidden in visually explicit inflection cues before first response.
- Independent first case-relation choice and separately typed target form. Save distinct evidence for **meaning-choice accuracy vs form accuracy**, not one aggregated "grammar knowledge" score.
- Teacher-proposed complete target form and explicit alternatives (empty until checked), semitransparent/transparent label, explanatory Ukrainian meaning after response. Exact S2-reviewed known wrong forms map to potential error families; **unknown typed error remains unclassified**, no regex suffix learner "diagnosis".
- New personal real-world situation for **independent full-sentence transfer**, no answer key and ungraded absent human S2 rubric; later true held-out different word/context/voice on days 14/30 prepared in separate frozen assessment bank, not training answer repetition.
- **Hard gate:** bank and *every* item have `editorial_draft_pending_S2_review`, `answerKeyReviewed=false` and `DRAFT_NEVER_AUTOSHIP`. Existing Web app does not load this JSON; production reference engine rejects it. The fact that forms appear plausible here **does not constitute human review**.

**Coverage:** 3 *kauppa* local case contrasts; *käsi→kädessä*, *vesi→vedessä*, *laukku→laukussa/laukkuun/laukusta*, *ovi→ovea/oven* negation and bounded object; *odottaa bussia*, *pitää kahvista*, *tutustua naapuriin* rektio; *vesi→vedeksi* transformation. Topic: everyday dwelling, store, travel, personal possessions. Additional lower-frequency changed stem types, plurals and register need S2-authored separate follow-up; 14 are a seed, not full B1 curriculum.

**Specific error feedback WITHOUT a hallucinated rule:**
- Meaning wrong, form right: learner may produce memorized "kaupassa" without reliably understanding missä/mihin/mistä; report choice separately and revisit meaning via genuinely changed scenario.
- Meaning right, form wrong; e.g. *käsessä* when expected *kädessä*: after independent answer show lemma and S2-verified change; classify "stem allomorphy" **only when explicitly in approved known-wrong map**, otherwise "unclassified".
- *En avaa oven*: negative bounded-object choice mismatch (expected *ovea*), but never diagnose a learner's general competence from one answer.
- Input that is S2-valid alternative must be accepted when declared by editor; dialect/colloquial forms cannot be universally auto-rejected by matching first key only.
- "Ти написав 3 речення" is **practice exposure**, never proof of B1 writing if no human rater. No production of artificial CEFR/YKI grade.

## 5. Controlled outcome study R08-A…R08-D — all NOT RUN

**R08-A/P0: meaning-first + form-first-try separation vs strong one-step exercise.** Same S2-approved Finnish lexemes, explicit case meanings, written answer time, replay, feedback and lesson dose in both. A does a separate *why/in what direction* choice before typing inflected form; B is a well-designed ordinary contextual cloze with equally detailed feedback. Primary **day14 unseen new lexeme morphological written production in a functional request**, blind scored; secondaries case interpretation, negative outcome (extra time/load), day30. Randomize and baseline stratify A2–B1; log actual practice time to ensure dose parity. Language transfer may be absent — report it.

**R08-B/P0: changed-stem visual cue vs same meaning in text.** Both after *first independent* response; A semantically focused lemma→allomorph→form diagram, B equivalent high-quality text explaining same change. Primary held-out changed-stem task after 14d, task response time and comprehension. Never leak key beforehand; no color-only result or mandatory sound.

**R08-C/P1: independent novel production vs matched trained-form repetition.** Equal time/exposure, identical input and feedback. A later asks different lemma and real situation; B gets additional repetition of studied form plus a new question matched for activity. Primary novel B1 writing or orally communicated meaning (human-rated) at 14/30d, not same-item recognition.

**R08-D/P1: rektio chunks vs case tables.** Both see same high-frequency real constructions and duration; A semantic full-sentence chunks (*pidän kahvista*, *tutustun naapuriin*) with independent production vs B structured reference-and-output drills. Separate measured comprehension and human-rated new message, avoid rewarding only textbook case names.

**Method gates:** freeze unique held-out vocabulary and approved keys BEFORE assignment, no learned item in blind test; R04 trained human judge/uncertainty on writing/speaking; preregister minimum meaningful improvement and power/sample/baseline/attrition/multiple outcomes; consent and privacy, novel voices as needed, post-lesson retention 14 and 30 days. An RCT outcome on trained pseudowords doesn't automatically show conversation fluency. No project human trial registered/recruited.

## 6. Acceptance gates R8-01…R8-18 (PROPOSED, not product-wide passed)

1. Every published form + all accepted alternatives reviewed by Finnish S2 language professional; editor review metadata and version required.
2. Meaning relation encoded separately from word-form spelling; semantic options support actual Finnish meaning, no accidental impossible distractor leakage.
3. Independent meaning choice precedes first inflected answer without source answer key/word-highlight revealing it.
4. First form+meaning independent scores immutable after repair; assistance separately labeled supported/ungraded.
5. Distinguish case selection vs stem alternation vs valency vs boundedness; never use overconfident regex-generated error label.
6. Changed stem variation tied to word/case: *vesi→vedessä* and *veteen*, *käsi→kädessä* are not one "always add -de" rule.
7. Correct alternatives and acceptable puhekieli register systematically vetted; normalization preserves Finnish length/ä/ö.
8. Exact form objective scored only for approved S2 structured response; creative writing/speaking ungraded absent calibrated R04 humans.
9. New-context transfer changes lemma/situation, hides worked keys and is held-out; independent meaningful communication requirement.
10. EXAM-only material and unofficial practice never conflated; no color/grammar answer cues in active timed EXAM.
11. R06 recommendation may target **observed confirmed errors**, not assumed Ukrainian-specific difficulty/error prevalence.
12. Audio, visual stem cue or animation opt-in/relevant; answer and morphological picture key shown only after independent response, no background music during phonetics.
13. Offline audio/image/right hash known, S2 accurate and pack verified; up-to-3–4GB Android is an **upper capacity** not mandated filler; plain accessible text fallback.
14. Responsive Finnish text accessibility, high contrast WCAG 2.2 AA, no color-only "case" signal, keyboard and 200% zoom.
15. Event logging first attempt atomic and migration-safe on Web Dexie/Android Room before runtime integration (existing MVP localStorage remains).
16. Contextual vocabulary frequency and B1 function checked; no unreviewed corpus/textbook copied into public app.
17. All tests/delayed language outcomes measured versus strong active equivalent condition with confidence bounds and harm attrition.
18. Dedicated Web/Android feature PR with S2 verified content, real browser, mobile UI, error recovery and rollback; research-only reference ≠ shipped feature.
