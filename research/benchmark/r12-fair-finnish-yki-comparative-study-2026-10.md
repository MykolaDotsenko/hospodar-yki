# R12 — об'єктивне порівняння Hospodar YKI з альтернативами: факти, цінність, доказ навчального результату

**Перевірено:** 28.10.2026. **Research owner:** research/benchmark; **product strategy owner:** [docs/28](../../docs/28-product-quality-niche-leadership-strategy.md). **Status:** source-based market review + independently-audited Hospodar code inventory + [machine-readable 2026-10 snapshot](r12-competitor-evidence-snapshot-2026-10.json) + [21 Node anti-hype tests](../../app/tests/r12-benchmark-evidence.test.mjs). **NO competitor accounts or premium installations, NO 320px/Android/TalkBack competitor test, NO controlled head-to-head participants, NO measured product superiority, NO official 2027 digital exam certification.** Adult Ukrainian-L1 Finn learners A2.2→B1+, target A1.3–B2.1 and YKI keskitaso four independent skills.

**Scope distinction:** Hospodar's target scientific architecture is substantially more extensive than the shipped Web MVP. "Planned 3–4GB fully offline Android", R05 human audio intake, R06 adaptation, R07 interactive task cycle, R08-MOT habit logic, R08 grammar drafts (14/14 S2 PENDING), R11 migration preflight **are NOT production features**. Current LEARN uses localStorage; current EXAM 9-item independent Web mock uses IndexedDB V1 and ungraded speaking/writing, strict 2027 UI fidelity OFF. We DO NOT market proposed capabilities as verified advantage. Price is dated market-specific self-report unless checked at live checkout; unknown is neither free nor absent.

## 1. Research decision and legitimate comparator

**PICOT:** For Ukrainian-speaking adults baseline A2–B1, if assigned to Hospodar vs a strong YKI-specific tool (e.g. Finnish it!, YKI Trainer) or the best viable **free resource bundle** (Yle YKItreenit + OPH demos), given equal active Finnish study minutes and matched practical themes, does Hospodar produce greater **day-14 independently completed novel functional Finnish writing** blind-rated by calibrated Finnish S2 judges? Secondary day30, listening to an unfamiliar natural voice, novel reading, consented oral performance, usability burden, accessibility and total € cost. Distinct EXAM accuracy endpoint: demonstrable agreement with **OPH digitesti demo behavior** on verified items, no invented grade.

**Result:** HOSPODAR COMPETITIVE EFFICACY = **UNKNOWN**, not 90/100 or first place. Eight external candidates' learning effects *relative to ours* also UNKNOWN. Product pages and catalog reviews establish features, price examples and visible limitations, not comparative randomized learning effects. The [R01 independent day14/30 pilot](../ux/learning-impact-evaluation-protocol.md) is NOT RUN; the head-to-head protocol below is **PROPOSED, NOT REGISTERED**.

**Immediate product decision:** **ADOPT/P0** curated, rights-cleared, S2-reviewed Finnish and honest first-answer evidence; **ADAPT/P0** real-world communicative topics, human natural/puhekieli audio and four-skill case-based learning, avoiding duplicated AI chatbot feature race; **RECHECK/P0** 2027 EXAM official behaviors at OPH live demos; **EXPERIMENT/P1** whether calm staged learning improves 14/30-day independent communication over active comparators; **REJECT/P0** claims "best app", "guaranteed YKI 3", unreviewed auto CEFR speaking score, deceptive prices, patented/copied competitor media.

## 2. Official reference, not a ninth commercial rival

**Opetushallitus (OPH)** [pre-test official page](https://www.oph.fi/fi/koulutus-ja-tutkinnot/ennen-yki-testia) links **four separate ungraded digital demos**: reading/listening/writing/speaking. [OPH announcement 18.09.2026](https://www.oph.fi/fi/uutiset/2026/yki-digitestia-kokeillaan-marraskuussa-2026) confirms Finnish keskitaso digital pilot on **07.11.2026** and gradual migration aimed at early 2027, with paper alternatives in Nov. As of 28 October, the future November pilot has **not** taken place. The previously logged Hospodar R02 text fetch was 403: NOT a full manual demo, and 403 is not proof a user cannot access. No competitor can self-certify being identical to finalized 2027 screen/time/recording controls on evidence gathered here. Marketing "official-like" is **not** verified fidelity.

**OPH must determine exam-specific benchmark**: task semantics, functional keyboard/mic controls, timed and replay behavior *only where actually confirmed*, accessibility arrangements and answer submission. Training LEARN affordances (caption, explanation, reward) are **not** silently carried into EXAM. Do not republish OPH assets.

## 4. What genuinely differentiates us, and what currently does not

**Cannot claim unique:** Ukrainian interface (Finnish it! already supports it), four YKI skills (Finnish it!, YKI Trainer, Lukla and Finnly list them), spaced repetition (Finnly), AI feedback (several), gamification (Duolingo/Finnly), human audio (Speakly/Yle) or a YKI-looking timer (YKI Trainer). We don't use fabricated "AI competitors are bad" narrative.

**Potential high-value advantage → exact release evidence:**

1. **P0 expert-authored and reliably edited Finnish:** source record, rights, S2 reviewer for every published key/variant/dialogue/puhekieli register; release-block invalid forms. Current original R08 drafts are **not approved**. Compare with independent S2 blinded error audits of a sample from each legitimately accessible product (no copying paywalled content).
2. **P0 transparent evidence of independent real-world transfer:** first attempt vs assisted answer vs guided repair vs human-reviewed productive work; calendar day14/30 *new* event, topic and voice. Current R06–R08 references not integrated; R01 human trial not run. No false "92% B1" or instant AI grades.
3. **P0 reliable offline-first full **Android** (if eventually built):** rights-cleared human/audio/video installed with Play Asset Delivery under total **4GB compressed** plus separate pack limits, verified contentHash and local Room persistence. Android pack not built. Web cache is separate and cannot be claimed equivalent.
4. **P0 faithful 2027 Digitesti:** use OPH four demos and verified current rules, compare functional focus/keyboard/audio/status/time on real browser, not appearance alone. Current strict 2027 fidelity OFF; no certification.
5. **P1 practical Ukrainian→Finnish meaning-first task flow:** work/childcare/housing/customer service, deliberate learning UX, controlled support/voice/silent design; but Ukrainian localization itself is not defensible moat. Compare human rubric on task usefulness, not first glance design.
6. **P1 pedagogically useful quiet visual/gameplay:** verify higher *delayed Finnish skill* vs matched plain controls, protect user autonomy; don't turn 2D asset size into proxy for instructional quality.

**Concrete Finnish scenario:** "Taloyhtiön huolto tulee torstaina kello 14." LEARN tests meaning first (when + who), new-speaker independent listening without captions, then optional Ukrainian feedback, written message "En ole kotona silloin. Sopiiko perjantai?" as **ungraded** until trained assessor, and new independent transfer with different building-service setting on day14. EXAM uses separate officially-matched fixed prompts with no hints/points, keeping speaking evidence human-ungraded. This scenario must be **S2-reviewed and rights-cleared before publication**, not borrowed verbatim from any competitor.

## 6. Reproducible product-level field audit (planned, NOT executed)

**Independent sampling:** 3 proficiency bands A2.2 / B1 / B1+ with baseline S2 placement; stratify by device and interface support language (Ukrainian vs English is a confound), work+family vs other contexts. Obtain informed consent and do not capture screenshots of personal writing/voice, payment credentials or proprietary paid tasks without rights. Avoid selection by how much users already like Hospodar.

**Two research questions, separate stages:**

- **R12-F (feature comparability and fair task audit):** 8 products, frozen public versions/platform and region, same physical phone and headphones, complete ethical 3 × 4-skill scenarios with genuine platform prompts where legally accessible (not copied) and report **not supported**, **not accessible**, **unknown**, **observed** distinctly. Independent observers record offline flight mode, 200% text, keyboard, unprompted task completion, Finnish naturalness under S2 reviewer and pricing. Source pages are not hands-on tests.
- **R12-L (causal learning package trial):** at minimum Hospodar vs one purpose-built YKI app and a fair **Yle + OPH demos** free alternative *bundle*; randomize adults within baseline proficiency and language interface, equal effective minutes over the same length, matched **learning goals and difficulty** without copying competitor copyrighted curricula. Strong active arms, capture what differs (media, hinting, feedback, instructor). **Primary:** blind human-rated day14 independent unseen **writing** to a functional recipient; secondaries new-voice listening, independent reading, consented human-scored speaking, day30. Register primary endpoint, minimum meaningful difference, power, multiple testing, missing-data policy and harm/attrition **before** first enrolment; neither sample size nor expected effect can responsibly be promised today.
- **R12-X (official digital fidelity):** inspect OPH four demos manually on supported device; enter details into existing [R02 fidelity source](../yki/20-yki-official-complete-audit-2026-10.md) with statuses `VERIFIED`/`UNKNOWN`, not inflated "matches" percentages. Compare Hospodar strict simulation separately and document each unsupported rule. Do not access real exam protected content or re-upload OPH demo assets.

**Evaluation panels:** separate S2 linguists for language naturalness/acceptance, trained blind written/spoken rubric raters (inter-rater agreement and adjudication), accessibility evaluator, privacy/data and product owner. No participant recruitment, payouts or studies are represented as underway.

## 8. 18 R12 acceptance gates (current report ≠ passed outcomes)

1. All competitors originate from public verified sources, legitimate role in Finnish/YKI market, no fabricated names.
2. Each feature has a specific evidence level (`vendor_claim`, `independent_catalog_review`, `developer_listing`, `own repo code`, `unknown`), not "tested" from brochure.
3. All eight alternatives and Hospodar represented, OPH official baseline not commercial app.
4. For each of four skills separate stated support from independently graded produced capability.
5. No causal superiority inferred from App Store stars, XP, claims, 2026 dashboard meta or raw feature totals.
6. Prices market/time/tier specific, no fabricated numeric EUR for hidden checkout.
7. Ukrainian interface among competitors explicitly acknowledged (Finnish it!), never called unique.
8. Human native voices and real puhekieli in Yle/Speakly acknowledged rather than claimed Hospodar-only.
9. Working LEARN/EXAM vs planned architecture and licensed/approved materials separately labelled.
10. User data privacy/security/offline "unknown" distinct from "no", do not collect private app account data by scraping.
11. 2027 official Exam UI fidelity remains `UNKNOWN` until actual OPH demo behavior verified.
12. Strong free competitor bundle includes authentic Yle plus official OPH demo, not only gamified A1 Duolingo.
13. Same device, baseline and effective practice time, tasks comparable in topic/difficulty, unequal curricula disclosed.
14. Writing and speaking independent S2 human rubric; no AI "B1 78%" treated as official certificate.
15. Preregister day14 primary and day30 secondary; report CI, null and dropouts, no manufactured effect sizes.
16. Competitor content copyright, terms and paid access respected; no rehosted tasks/media/screenshots without right.
17. 100-point scores, leaderboards and claims "best" **blocked** until observed complete matched evidence; `null` before trials.
18. Result is versioned, testable, cross-linked to product strategy docs/28 and updated after real official/market changes.

**Conclusion:** today's valuable result is a **truthful product differentiator map and empirical test design**, not a winner's podium. Hospodar's biggest advantage is still a **hypothesis** (expert-reviewed, rights-cleared, authentic Finnish + measured durable transfer + legal offline YKI simulation) and its biggest risk is presenting reference code as a finished flagship product.
